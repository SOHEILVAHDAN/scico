# THE CITY THAT BREATHES — TEHRAN
### Photorealistic Cinematic Short Film — Director's Script & Shotlist
**Format:** 90-second hero film | 8K HDR | 2.39:1 anamorphic | 24fps | Dolby Atmos  
**Style:** Apple Keynote polish × Tesla launch energy × National Geographic realism  
**Mood Arc:** Dark → Hope → Future  
**Never:** CGI-looking, game-engine, cartoonish. Must feel like real location footage shot on ARRI Alexa 65.

**Camera:** Locked. 
**Lighting:** N/A.

#### SCENE 2: AERIAL DRONE OVER TEHRAN — SMOG [0:04-0:10]
**Visual:** Real drone footage, 120m altitude over central Tehran. Alborz mountains barely visible through brown haze. Aging concrete towers, Chamran/Hemmat highway interchange. Dust motes in sunlight, volumetric haze. Faint floating holographic labels drifting like dust: `NOx · SO₂ · VOC · CO₂ · PM2.5` (subtle, 20% opacity, light type tracking particles).
**Camera:** Slow push-in aerial, 12mm lens, slight yaw drift, gimbal stabilized.

**Sound:** Dark ambient drone, traffic bed, muffled breathing onset.

#### SCENE 3: FIRST-PERSON WALK — STREET LEVEL [0:10-0:15]

**Visual:** In deep background of chaotic street, one 10m long x 3m high bright white concrete wall stands out — impossibly clean against grime. Sun hits it. Slow dolly-in (50m → 15m), focus rack from foreground chaos (bokeh) to pristine wall texture sharp.

**Sound:** Traffic starts to fade -6dB, bird call enters faint.

#### SCENE 5: PARTICLES DRIFTING [0:20-0:25]
**Visual:** 2m from wall. Airborne pollution particles visibly drifting toward wall surface in sunbeams (practical volumetric). Photoreal wall surface with micro-pitting visible.

**Sound:** Traffic -18dB, wind chime, single sparrow, hopeful pad.

#### SCENE 6: TIME FREEZE — NOx MOLECULE [0:25-0:30]
**Visual:** Time freeze VFX — cars frozen mid-motion blur, pedestrian mid-step, leaf suspended. Photoreal, not CG. Only one ultra-detailed NOx molecule (scientifically accurate — N blue, O red, bond angle 134°) continues drifting in sharp focus toward wall. Subtle glow.
**Camera:** 50mm locked after freeze, slight parallax drift.
**Sound:** All diegetic stops, low frequency drop, single high tone.

#### SCENE 7: SEAMLESS MACRO DIVE INTO CONCRETE [0:30-0:35]
**Visual:** Invisible transition from wall surface into microstructure. Camera dives through surface: cement crystals (calcium silicate hydrate needles), scattered pitch-black activated carbon particles (porous), and bright anatase TiO2 photocatalyst nanoparticles (<25nm) catching light from within. Lit by internal sunlight scattering. 8K scanning electron realism but cinematic.
**Camera:** Continuous virtual macro probe, 0.01mm travel, focus breathing real.
**Sound:** Deep whoosh, then crystalline sparkle.

#### SCENE 8: PHOTOCATALYST ACTIVATION [0:35-0:41]
**Visual:** Pure elegant molecular cinematography. Sun photon hits TiO2, electron excitation visual (golden flash), reactive oxygen species (hydroxyl radicals) form as glowing halos. NOx molecule collides, breaks apart into harmless nitrates. No text. Scientifically credible, beautiful.
**Camera:** 360° orbit around reaction at nanosecond scale.
**Light:** Internal ray-traced sunlight. 
**Sound:** Subtle orchestral swell starts, strings.

#### SCENE 9: CARBON PORE NETWORK [0:41-0:46]

### Sound Design Bible
- **Act 1 Dark (0:00-0:15):** Tehran field recordings (contact mics), dark ambient pad in D minor 48Hz, coughing, siren filtered, breathing motif.
- **Act 2 Hope (0:15-0:51):** Transition — high-pass filter opening, bird call (Acrocephalus recorded in Tehran), strings swell (hybrid: real orchestra + subtle synth), photocatalyst = crystalline bell textures (granular).
- **Act 3 Future (0:51-1:15):** Full orchestra in D → A major, choir pad, percussion (taikos soft), children, final single breath recorded on Neumann U87.

### VFX Rules — Never CGI-looking
1. All particles are practical vol capture referenced from smoke machine in warehouse, comped with ray-traced light
2. Molecule = MD simulation (GROMACS) rendered in Houdini with spectral refraction, not glowing cartoon
3. Macro concrete = real SEM scans → photogrammetry → displacement, not procedural invention
4. Time freeze = high-resolution plate array (15 Reds), not interpolation

### Color Pipeline
- Look 1 Dark: Kodak 2383 print emulation, desat -20, shadows + brown
- Look 2 Hope: Clean white point 6200K, highlights soft bloom
- Look 3 Future: Tehran Blue (Pantone 7455C) — vibrant, high dynamic 1000 nits sky
